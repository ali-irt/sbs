Our Story

Passion for
Pure Beauty

Shine by Shania was born from a deep belief that what we put on our hair matters just as much as what we put in our bodies. Founded by Shania Raza, the brand emerged from personal struggle with hair health and a relentless search for truly natural solutions.

After discovering the transformative power of organic ingredients — amla from ancient Ayurveda, pure aloe vera, cold-pressed olive oil — Shania decided to share these gifts with the world. Every product is formulated with intention, love and an unwavering commitment to purity.

Today, Shine by Shania is trusted by thousands of customers, each discovering that the most beautiful hair comes from the most natural ingredients.
